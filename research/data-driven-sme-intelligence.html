Research Questions

RQ1

What minimum data volume and quality thresholds are required for machine learning models to generate reliable operational insights in typical Startup and Micro-Business digital environments?

RQ2

Which machine learning methodologies — supervised classification, clustering, anomaly detection, or time-series forecasting — produce the most actionable and interpretable outputs for non-specialist Startup and Micro-Business operators?

RQ3

To what extent does the provision of natural language explanations alongside ML model outputs increase Startup and Micro-Business operator confidence in, and adoption of, data-driven recommendations?
