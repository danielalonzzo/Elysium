SYMPTOM GUIDE

When something changes,
every sign counts.

Answer four questions to decide the next step. The result indicates urgency and is not a diagnosis.

Do not give human medicines. In case of breathing difficulty, collapse or significant bleeding, seek immediate care.
QUESTION 1 OF 4
Warning signs

Is there any sign that needs immediate attention?

Difficulty breathing, blue or very pale gums, collapse, loss of consciousness, seizures, heavy bleeding, a serious injury or possible poisoning.
