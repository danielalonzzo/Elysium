SYMPTOM GUIDE

When something changes,
every sign counts.

Answer four quick questions to determine the next step. This result serves as initial triage and does not replace in-person veterinary assessment.

Never administer human medications to animals. In case of severe breathing distress, collapse or acute bleeding, seek emergency care immediately.
QUESTION 1 OF 4
Warning signs

Is there any sign that needs immediate attention?

Difficulty breathing, blue or very pale gums, collapse, loss of consciousness, seizures, heavy bleeding, serious injury or possible poisoning.

Myth or truth?

“If my dog’s nose is dry, it has a fever.”

Not quite. It’s a myth.

Nose moisture changes naturally throughout the day. The only reliable way to check temperature is with a clinical thermometer.
