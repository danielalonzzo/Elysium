How it works

  1. Drop all the PDFs you want to combine above, or add them one by one with “+ Add PDF”.
  2. Drag the pages — or whole groups of selected pages — into the final order. Each file has its own letter and colour so you can tell them apart.
  3. Write a name for the file and press Download PDF.

Order exactly as you want

You are not limited to putting one file after another: you can interleave pages from different files, remove covers or blank pages and rotate scanned sheets before saving.

Private by design

Merging happens on your device; the workspace requires a verified account and an active license. Your PDF files are not uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

How many PDFs can I merge?

As many as your device can handle; each file can be up to 200 MB.

Will the merged PDF keep its text and links?

Yes. Pages are copied as they are, so text remains selectable and searchable.

Can I merge PDFs on my phone?

Yes. On a touch screen, hold a page for a moment and drag it to reorder.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

Not to edit them: merging, reordering, rotating, deleting and extracting pages happen inside your browser and the file never leaves your device. Only “Convert to Word” sends the edited PDF to the Elysium server, which deletes it as soon as the Word file exists.

Is it free?

Yes, with no ads, watermarks; a verified account and an approved free license are required. Word conversion allows 10 documents per hour per visitor so the service stays available.
