      How it works

      1. Drop the PDF above. Each page appears with its number.
      2. Drag a page to its new place. If several pages are selected, they move together. On a phone, hold a page for a moment before dragging.
      3. Press Download PDF to save the new order.

      Drag, keyboard or buttons

      Use whatever is most comfortable: drag with the mouse, use the ‹ and › buttons on each page, or focus a page and press Alt + arrow keys to move it.

      Across several files

      Add more PDFs to the editor and you can mix their pages in a single new order before saving.

      Frequently asked questions

      Can I move several pages at once?

      Yes. Select them first; dragging one of them moves the whole selection.

      Does it work on a phone or tablet?

      Yes. Hold a page until it lifts, then drag it.

      Can I undo a move?

      Yes. Undo goes back step by step.

      Are my PDF files uploaded?

      Not to edit them: merging, reordering, rotating, deleting and extracting pages happen inside your browser and the file never leaves your device. Only “Convert to Word” sends the edited PDF to the Elysium server, which deletes it as soon as the Word file exists.

      Is it free?

      Yes, with no ads, watermarks or sign-up. Word conversion allows 10 documents per hour per visitor so the service stays available.
