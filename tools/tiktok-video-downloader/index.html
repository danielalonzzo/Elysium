How it works

  1. In TikTok, open the video, tap Share → Copy link. Short links like vm.tiktok.com work too.
  2. Paste the link above. You will see the available qualities — usually 540p, 720p or 1080p.
  3. Choose MP4 for the video or MP3/M4A for the sound, and press Download.

Without the watermark

The file you get is the video stream TikTok delivers to its own player, not the copy it generates for sharing, so it has no watermark. Use it to keep your own videos, repost your content elsewhere or edit it.

Quality and format

TikTok publishes several versions of each video. When it offers both, the downloader prefers H.264 for maximum compatibility; the highest quality can be HEVC (H.265), which plays on iPhone, Mac and most modern devices.

Frequently asked questions

Does the creator know I downloaded the video?

No. Downloading a public video does not notify anyone. Even so, only download videos you own or may use.

Can I download TikTok photo slideshows?

Not yet. The downloader works with videos; posts made only of photos are not supported.

Can I get only the sound of a TikTok?

Yes. Choose MP3 or M4A and you will get the audio of that video.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads or paid subscriptions. The tools are for Elysium clients: sign in with your Elysium account, with nothing to request. Fair-use limits keep the service available for everyone: 30 link checks, 10 downloads and 10 Word conversions per hour.

Can I download any video?

Only public videos. Content that is private, requires signing in, is age-restricted or is a live stream in progress cannot be downloaded. Download only videos you own or have permission to use.
