      How it works

      1. Drop the PDF above and look through the thumbnails.
      2. Click the pages to remove and press Delete — or use the × on a single page. Undo brings them back.
      3. Press Download PDF to save the document without those pages.

      A clean copy to share

      Frequently asked questions

      Is my original PDF changed?

      No. You download a new PDF; the original file stays as it was.

      Can I delete many pages at once?

      Yes. Use Select all or Shift-click to select a range, then press Delete.

      Can I recover a deleted page?

      Yes, with Undo, as long as you have not reset the editor.

      Are my PDF files uploaded?

      Not to edit them: merging, reordering, rotating, deleting and extracting pages happen inside your browser and the file never leaves your device. Only “Convert to Word” sends the edited PDF to the Elysium server, which deletes it as soon as the Word file exists.

      Is it free?

      Yes, with no ads, watermarks or sign-up. Word conversion allows 10 documents per hour per visitor so the service stays available.
