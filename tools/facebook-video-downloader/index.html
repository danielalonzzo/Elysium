Downloads

    Download only your own content or content you have permission to use.

    How it works

    1. On Facebook, open the video, choose Share → Copy link (or copy the address from your browser).
    2. Paste the link above and wait a moment while the available qualities load.
    3. Choose the quality or MP3/M4A and press Download.

    Public videos only

    The downloader works with videos anyone can watch without signing in: posts from public pages, public profiles and Facebook Watch. Videos shared only with friends or inside private groups are not available.

    Quality

    Facebook often keeps separate versions in standard and high definition. You choose between the ones that exist for that video; when the image and sound come separately, they are joined into a single MP4.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I download videos from private groups?

    No. Only videos that are public on Facebook can be downloaded.

    Why does a Facebook video fail?

    It may be private, deleted, region-restricted or require signing in. Try another public link.

    Is the downloaded video the original quality?

    It is the best version Facebook makes available publicly for that video, without re-encoding when MP4 is possible.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. There are no ads, subscriptions or accounts. Fair-use limits keep the service available for everyone: 30 link checks, 10 downloads and 10 Word conversions per hour.

    Can I download any video?

    Only public videos. Content that is private, requires signing in, is age-restricted or is a live stream in progress cannot be downloaded. Download only videos you own or have permission to use.
