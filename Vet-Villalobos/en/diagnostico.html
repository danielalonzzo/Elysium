PUT YOUR KNOWLEDGE TO THE TEST

Myth or truth?

0 of 6 answered
01

“If my dog’s nose is dry, it has a fever.”

Not quite. It’s a myth.

Nose moisture changes throughout the day and says nothing about temperature. The only reliable way is a thermometer, at the clinic.

02

“One dog year equals seven human years.”

Not quite. It’s a myth.

The first two years count for much more, and after that it depends on size. Try the age calculator on this page.

03

“Grapes and raisins can damage a dog’s kidneys.”

Not quite. It’s true.

They can cause serious kidney injury, and no amount is known to be safe. If your dog ate some, call the clinic even if it looks fine.

04

“If my cat never goes outside, it doesn’t need the vet.”

Not quite. It’s a myth.

Cats are very good at hiding discomfort. The yearly check-up catches dental, weight or kidney problems in time, and decides which vaccinations they need.

05

“Household paracetamol (acetaminophen) is dangerous for cats.”

Not quite. It’s true.

It is toxic to them even in small doses. Never give human medicines to a pet without veterinary advice.

06

“Milk is good for adult cats.”

Not quite. It’s a myth.

Many adult cats tolerate lactose poorly and milk can give them diarrhoea. What they need is fresh water, always available.
