Veterinary services

The logo says it without words.

A cat and a dog, each in its own window, inside the same circle. Every pet has its own needs, and all of them get the same care: that is Villalobos in a nutshell.

Consultations, vaccinations, pharmacy, grooming, pet shop, pet hotel and export paperwork, at four clinics in Heredia. Services, opening hours and availability are confirmed directly with each clinic.
