The Post-Cancer Challenge

Today, thousands of people survive cancer in Portugal. But when treatment ends, a new problem begins: most patients lack structured support to recover their physical, emotional, and functional lives.


Hospitals treat the disease. But almost no one treats the recovery.

The result is visible.

Chronic fatigue, loss of mobility, depression, isolation, physical disability, and difficulty returning to normal life.

ONCORE was born to fill this void.

The ONCORE Solution

A multidisciplinary space that unites all fundamental capabilities. We do not treat tumours. We treat the recovery of the person.

Physiotherapy

Physiotherapy focused on mobility rehabilitation and pain reduction.

Osteopathy

Nutrition

Psycho-oncology

Continuous emotional support throughout the entire rehabilitation process.

Performance

Functional recovery and maximisation of quality of life post-cancer.

Intersection of health and longevity

Our goal is not just to increase survival. It is to restore functionality, identity, and future.

Because surviving cancer cannot be the end of the journey. It must be the beginning of a new life.
