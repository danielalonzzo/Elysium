Myth or truth?

0 of 6 answered
01

“If my dog’s nose is dry, it has a fever.”

Not quite. It’s a myth.

Nose moisture changes throughout the day and says nothing about temperature. The only reliable measurement is with a clinical thermometer at the practice.
