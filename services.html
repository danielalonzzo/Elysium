Transparency & Licensing

01

Zero Development Cost

Elysium assumes 100% of the initial engineering. Your subscription ensures your system evolves without becoming obsolete.

02

Code Ownership (IP)

The source code is the intellectual property of Elysium λ. The client acquires a full use license linked to their active subscription.

03

Lifecycle

If the subscription is interrupted, the system and its support are automatically suspended to protect the infrastructure.

04

Valuation and Transfer

We offer audits for code purchase if your model requires it. After purchase, maintaining base support (Hosting) is required.
