Transparency & Licensing

  • Zero CapEx (No Development Cost): Elysium assumes 100% of the initial engineering and development. Your subscription ensures your system evolves and never becomes obsolete.
  • Code Ownership (IP): The source code remains the exclusive intellectual property of Elysium λ Development & Research. You acquire a full-use licence tied to your active subscription.
  • Lifecycle & Continuity: If the subscription is interrupted, the system and its associated technical support will be suspended.
  • Valuation & Buyout: If your business model requires total code ownership, we offer an auditing and valuation process for project buyout. (Note: Upon acquiring the code, maintenance by Elysium is deactivated unless a separate maintenance plan is contracted).
