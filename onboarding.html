Module 1 λ 01 · Organization

Organization & Contacts *

Welcome to the λ Immersion Protocol. Let's confirm who we are building for and who leads the project on your side.

Operational Telemetry *

To engineer your infrastructure we need to map your real daily operation — not the ideal one, the actual one.

Example: "A WhatsApp message arrives → I write it in a notebook → I copy it into Excel on Fridays → I invoice at the end of the month."

Team & Ergonomics *

We design ergonomic interfaces adapted to human behaviour. Tell us who will actually use the system every day.

If the level is low, we prioritize large buttons, strict linear flows, and avoid complex dashboards.

Technical Inventory *

State of the art: what already exists, who controls it, and which data would need to be migrated.

We never ask for passwords here. We only need to know what exists and who holds the keys, to plan the transfer.

        Problem & Measurable Objective *

        Critical variables and problem definition — the core that the new infrastructure must resolve.

        Examples: "20 more bookings per month", "zero hours of weekly Excel work", "15 qualified leads per month". This becomes the acceptance criterion of the QA phase.

        Security Architecture (RBAC) *

        This matrix translates directly into the access-control rules of your system: each person sees only what they need to see.

        Functional Scope & Integrations *

        Preliminary architecture: which capabilities the ecosystem must include and which third-party services it must talk to.

        Identity & Design *

        Visual direction for the interface your clients and team will live in.

        Operations & Launch *

        Delivery logistics: who operates the system, when it must go live, and who validates it.

        Privacy & Consent *

        Last step — then engineering begins.

        Your data will only be used to design, build, and maintain your digital infrastructure. You can request access, rectification, or deletion of your data at any time.
