## The four tiers

They are **cumulative**: each one contains the one above it. Annual billing is
priced at ten months, so a year costs two months less than twelve monthly
payments.

The mistake to avoid: **online booking, client records, the private client
area, the administrator CRM, online payments and occupancy reporting belong to
Ecosystem, not to Presence.** If asked whether a booking system or a client
portal is included, the answer is Ecosystem.

## Research

Two projects are published, and their state matters more than their titles:

- *Ontology for digital inclusion and service access in rural environments* —
  pilot study. Research question, objectives, methodology and data sources are
  published.
- *Data-Driven Small Business and Startup Intelligence* — concept phase. The
  research questions and references are published; **the body sections are not
  written and there are no results.** Do not summarise findings it does not
  have.
