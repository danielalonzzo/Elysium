Research

DEVELOPMENT & RESEARCH

Applied Research Lines

Exploring the empirical intersection between Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), data topology, and structural cryptography.

🧠

Cognitive Ergonomics and Human-Computer Interaction

Empirical analysis of perceived latency and cognitive load in digital environments. We investigate asynchronous rendering methodologies and algorithmic mutation (Optimistic UI) to drastically reduce operational friction in complex transactional flows.

📊

Data Structuring and Operational Telemetry

Exploration of NoSQL architectures and high-concurrency information flow modelling. The objective is to translate massive volumes of operational data into deterministic dashboards and highly interpretable ecosystems (CRMs) for decision-making.

🌐

Applied Cryptography and High-Availability Systems

Study of event-driven network topologies (Serverless) and algorithmic vulnerability mitigation. We develop ecosystems that ensure strict regulatory compliance (Compliance-by-Design) without compromising computational efficiency or energy performance.
